Measuring Unmeasurability

Tim Chee | May 1, 2026 | Cambridge Part IIB Dissertation

Evidence of strategic vagueness in Singaporean political communication. I build a signal model of vague speech, construct a measure called semantic dispersion using LLM paraphrase distance (validated at 92.3% agreement with human raters), and test it on 62,169 hedged Singapore parliamentary claims. A redistricting shock and the Nominated MP institution provide causal identification.
